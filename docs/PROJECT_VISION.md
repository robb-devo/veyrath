# Project vision

**VEYRATH — UNBOUND WORLD.** A fantasy game built on Minecraft Java Edition through a custom
mod and a curated modpack. Singleplayer comes first. Multiplayer stays compatible where
reasonably possible.

This summarises the owner's Master Development Directive (2026-11-02), which remains the
authority. The owner may change the vision at any time. When that happens, update this file
first.

## Core vision

Minecraft is the technical platform, not the design goal. VEYRATH should feel like an entirely
different fantasy game, with its own visual identity, world generation, environments, rendering
character, gameplay, combat, animation, audio, weapons, bosses, creatures and progression. The
vanilla look is **not** an objective.

## Quality philosophy

There is no artificial time pressure. VEYRATH prefers:
- One exceptional system over five mediocre ones.
- Thorough engineering over rushed implementation.
- Designed architecture over accumulated workarounds.
- Proper testing over assumptions.
- Exceptional visuals over feature counts.
- Long-term maintainability over short-term convenience.

Effort goes where it improves the experience, not into polishing insignificant details.

## The world (Chapter 01)

One enormous, continuous Overworld with entirely custom geography:
- Enormous mountain ranges and extreme elevation differences.
- Deep valleys and gigantic ravines, massive waterfalls.
- Colossal forests and extraordinary trees.
- Floating islands as part of the Overworld.
- Huge rock formations, complex caves, distinct atmospheric regions.
- Rare, spectacular landmarks.

Regions are large and coherent. Biomes do not change every few hundred blocks.

**Art direction:** high fantasy (primary), dark fantasy (contrast and mystery) and surreal
fantasy (impossible geography). Bright, peaceful, almost heavenly regions sit alongside
ancient, threatening and alien ones. The world should keep producing memorable vistas.

**Six landscape pillars:**
- **Aethercrown Highlands**: monumental mountains.
- **Vesperfall Riftlands**: ravines and canyons.
- **Elderbough Expanse**: colossal forests.
- **Aetherreach**: floating islands.
- **Prismmere Wilds**: crystal and magic landscapes.
- **Cinderwake Dominion**: dark volcanic land.

They are developed into a fuller biome ecosystem in [BIOME_CATALOG.md](BIOME_CATALOG.md).

**Landmarks come in four tiers:** ordinary but beautiful terrain, unusual local formations,
major regional landmarks, and extremely rare world landmarks. Rarity protects their impact.

## Later Chapters (not authorized yet)

- **02 Player controller and combat.** Custom movement, camera and animation. Action-RPG
  combat with anime-inspired intensity (Black Clover, One Punch Man as references for
  direction): combos, dodge and dash, block and parry, air combat, abilities and ultimates.
  Clean and purposeful, not a pile of mechanics.
- **03 Weapons and audio.** Four to five weapons, each with its own model, animation set,
  rhythm, combos, abilities, effects and sound. High-quality licensed audio and dynamic music.
- **04 Bosses.** Two exceptionally polished bosses with phases, telegraphing, custom arenas and
  audiovisual presentation.
- **05 Dragon.** One exceptional dragon: summoning, arrival, landing, mounting, ground and
  flight movement, flight camera.
- **Future.** Structures, cities, dungeons, NPCs, quests, interface, progression, creatures and
  world events.

## Platform stance

- A powerful gaming PC is acceptable. Inefficient engineering is not.
- Respect licences.
- Our gameplay and world-generation systems are our own work. External tools may provide
  rendering, performance and asset infrastructure.
